Hand-In and Collection

Hand in laptops needing school setup by Wednesday 7 July 2027.

Laptop hand-in

First, complete the Year 9 Laptop Registration Form. We will then email you a DocuSign agreement. Only drop off the laptop after you have signed the agreement.

What should parents complete first?

Parents should complete the Year 9 Laptop Registration Form before the laptop is handed in. The Google Form registers the device and starts the DocuSign agreement process; it is not the agreement itself.

After you submit the form, we will email you the DocuSign agreement. You must sign it before bringing the laptop to either school office.

Complete Registration Form

Where can families hand in the laptop?

Please leave the laptop and charger at either the Prep School office or the Senior School office by Wednesday 7 July 2027. IT will collect laptops from both offices for setup.

Is an appointment needed?

Once you have signed the agreement, you can drop off the laptop without an appointment. Devices will be processed in the order they are received.

What must be handed in?

  • Laptop.
  • Charger.
  • Stylus or digital pen, if already purchased.
  • Any required pen charger or cable.
  • Laptop bag, if used.

Collection

The school will let you know when and where to collect the laptop, or whether your child will receive it when they return in September.

Late hand-in

Late devices will still be processed, but the school may not be able to guarantee they are ready for the start of term. Devices handed in by the deadline will be prioritised.
